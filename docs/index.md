# Welcome page

## Course information
The course is the seminar “Einführung in wissenschaftliches Arbeiten für eine Nachhaltige Entwicklung” (Proseminar) within Module 1 “Grundlagen Nachhaltiger Entwicklung” of the Bachelor Minor Sustainable Development (BSc Mi NE) at the University of Bern, offered in the autumn semester 2026. It complements the lecture “Einführung in die Herausforderungen und Grundlagen Nachhaltiger Entwicklung” and focuses on scientific working methods for sustainable development. Students first work through an online learning module on scientific research and writing, then join a thematic deepening group (max. five students) to explore a sustainability-related research topic using problem-oriented learning, including an expert exchange. The course culminates in a group research pitch presentation, a written group paper, and an individual reflection. It is aimed at students enrolled in the Bachelor Minor Sustainable Development, particularly those in Module 1, with varying prior knowledge of sustainable development and scientific research methods.

## Course learning outcomes
- Students know the scientific and epistemological foundations of discussions on sustainable development, as well as inter- and transdisciplinary methodological approaches to analyzing its challenges, and can reflect on them.
- Students can place concepts of sustainable development in the context of global development debates and derive research questions and challenges, including the role of science.
- Students can independently work on a sustainable development topic, present the results, and reflect on the disciplinary contribution and inter-/transdisciplinary character of the topic.
- Students know basic tools of scientific work, such as hypothesis formation, scientific argumentation, literature research, and evaluation of methodological approaches, and can apply them to their own work.
