# Welcome page

## Course information
This Proseminar, “Einführung in wissenschaftliches Arbeiten für eine Nachhaltige Entwicklung,” is part of Module 1 “Grundlagen Nachhaltiger Entwicklung” in the Bachelor Minor Sustainable Development (BSc Mi NE) and is offered in the autumn semester 2026. It introduces students to scientific working in the field of sustainable development through an online self-learning module, group-based thematic deepening on selected sustainability topics, expert exchange, and a research pitch presentation. The course is aimed at students of the Bachelor Minor Sustainable Development, including those with varying disciplinary backgrounds and prior knowledge of sustainable development and scientific research methods.

## Course learning outcomes
- Students know scientific and epistemological foundations of discussions on sustainable development, as well as inter- and transdisciplinary methodological approaches for analyzing sustainability challenges, and can reflect on them.
- Students can contextualize concepts of sustainable development within global development debates and derive research questions, challenges, and the role of science from these discussions.
- Students are able to work independently on a topic related to sustainable development, present the results, and reflect on the disciplinary contribution as well as the inter- and transdisciplinary character of the topic.
- Students know basic tools of scientific work, such as hypothesis formation, scientific argumentation, literature research, and evaluation of methodological approaches, and can apply them to their own work.
