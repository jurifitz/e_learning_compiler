# Welcome page

## Course information
The course is the seminar “Einführung in wissenschaftliches Arbeiten für eine Nachhaltige Entwicklung” (Proseminar) within Module 1 “Grundlagen Nachhaltiger Entwicklung” of the Bachelor Minor Nachhaltige Entwicklung, offered in the autumn semester 2026. It is designed for students of the Bachelor Minor Nachhaltige Entwicklung who are beginning the minor and need to develop basic competencies in scientific research, critical reading, argumentation, and interdisciplinary work on sustainable development topics. The seminar combines an online self-study module on scientific working, thematic group work on current sustainable development challenges, expert input, and a research pitch presentation. Students work in groups of up to five, prepare a written group paper, and submit an individual reflection.

## Course learning outcomes
- Students know the scientific and societal discussion around sustainable development and its associated challenges.
- Students recognize the normative character of sustainable development and the resulting different conceptual approaches.
- Students know scientific and epistemological foundations of the discussion on sustainable development.
- Students know interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary methodological approaches for analyzing sustainable development challenges and can reflect on them.
- Students can place sustainable development concepts in the context of global development discussions and derive research questions and challenges for science.
- Students can independently work on a sustainable development topic, present the results, and reflect on the disciplinary contribution and inter-/transdisciplinary character of the topic.
- Students know basic tools of scientific work, such as hypothesis formation, scientific argumentation, literature research, and evaluation of methodological approaches, and can apply them to their own work.
